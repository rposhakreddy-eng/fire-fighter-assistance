Thermal Image Analysis & Heat Palettes

Color palette transformation, thermal gradients, and engineering technical disclaimers.

IMPORTANT SCIENTIFIC & TECHNICAL NOTICE

Thermal-image detection has not been validated for this model. A standard RGB camera and a radiometric thermal camera are not equivalent. Standard YOLO models identify spatial contours and shapes rather than calibrated infrared surface temperatures. Surface temperatures are not measured or estimated without a calibrated radiometric sensor. Drones cannot see through solid concrete or steel structures.

Rescue Guidance & Fire Survival Safety Protocol

Conservative, safety-focused emergency instructions for victims and fire crews.

CRITICAL SAFETY DIRECTIVE

General guidance only — always follow official emergency responders' instructions. Automated software cannot assess invisible toxic gas levels, structural collapse hazards, or flashover conditions. Never instruct victims to pass through flames or dense smoke.

IMMEDIATE SURVIVAL

Actions for Trapped Victims

  • Call Emergency Services Immediately: Dial 911 / 112 / 101. Inform dispatchers of your exact room, floor, and visible landmarks.
  • Stay Low to the Ground: Smoke, heat, and carbon monoxide rise. Crawl on hands and knees where breathable air remains cleanest.
  • Check Doors Before Opening: Feel the door and doorknob with the back of your hand. If hot to the touch, DO NOT open it. Fire is on the other side.
  • Seal Gaps: Use wet towels, blankets, or clothing along door cracks and air vents to block smoke intrusion.
  • Signal Rescuers: Wave a bright flashlight, phone screen, or light-colored cloth at exterior windows. Shout at regular intervals.
  • NEVER Use Elevators: Elevators can stall due to electrical failure or open directly onto fire-involved floors.
TACTICAL CREWS

Drone Assisted Firefighter SOP

  • Primary Search Prioritization: Direct initial search teams toward verified high-confidence person detections with upper-body or limb signatures.
  • Thermal Differential Analysis: Compare ambient structural temperature with detected signatures to differentiate living victims from residual heat sources.
  • Two-In, Two-Out Rule: Never send an isolated responder into a smoke-filled quadrant based solely on drone telemetry. Maintain buddy team contact.
  • Thermal Sensor Limitations: Standard infrared does not penetrate double-pane low-E glass, water curtains, or reflective metallic vapor barriers.
